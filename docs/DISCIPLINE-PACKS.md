# HASHIRA Discipline Packs

## Core idea
HASHIRA should use one universal human-movement foundation plus modular discipline packs. The same athlete can train across multiple disciplines without creating disconnected programming systems.

## Universal Human Movement Foundation
Common concepts shared across disciplines include:
- squat
- hinge
- push
- pull
- carry
- locomotion
- rotation
- anti-rotation
- brace
- reach
- crawl
- jump
- land
- balance
- gait

These are movement concepts, not standalone commercial disciplines.

The universal layer should also understand:
- joint actions
- muscle groups
- movement planes
- stability and mobility demands
- coordination
- impact
- fatigue
- recovery cost
- progression relationships
- sequencing constraints

## Initial deep discipline packs

### 1. Calisthenics
The calisthenics pack should understand:
- relative strength
- bodyweight loading
- leverage
- assistance
- range of motion
- tempo
- stability
- static holds
- skill prerequisites
- body-line control
- progression trees

Core progression families may eventually include:
- push-up
- pull-up
- dip
- row
- squat / single-leg squat
- handstand
- L-sit
- hanging core

Progression must not be reduced to doing more reps. Difficulty may change through leverage, assistance, range, tempo, stability, or skill complexity.

### 2. Gym / Resistance Training
The gym pack should understand:
- external load
- free weights
- machines
- cables
- sets and reps
- RPE / RIR
- rest intervals
- training volume
- frequency
- exercise order
- progressive overload
- strength
- hypertrophy
- power
- muscular endurance

It must support multiple valid prescriptions based on outcome rather than hard-coding simplistic rules such as one universal rep range.

### 3. Mobility / Flexibility
The mobility pack should distinguish:
- mobility
- flexibility
- active range of motion
- passive range of motion
- joint preparation
- dynamic mobility
- static stretching
- loaded mobility
- recovery work

Mobility must not become generic cooldown filler. A mobility session may itself be the primary training objective.

### 4. Pilates / Core Control
The Pilates pack should preserve its own logic rather than being treated as light resistance training.

It should understand concepts such as:
- breathing
- control
- precision
- trunk organization
- alignment
- stability
- lever progression
- coordination
- flow
- quality of movement

Prescription may use repetitions, holds, breath cycles, range, control targets, and sequence quality rather than gym-style loading logic.

## Multi-discipline athletes
HASHIRA should not force every athlete into one permanent discipline. The future athlete model may support a training mix such as:

```text
Calisthenics   40%
Gym            25%
Mobility       20%
Pilates        15%
```

This is a planning concept, not a required V1 UI.

Optional training philosophies may include:
- balanced hybrid
- strength emphasis
- movement quality emphasis
- skill emphasis
- athletic emphasis

The programming engine should use these as weighting signals rather than rigid quotas.

## Discipline pack contract
Every future pack should define:
1. What outcomes the discipline trains well.
2. Its movement families.
3. Its progression logic.
4. Its prescription model.
5. Its sequencing rules.
6. Its fatigue/recovery characteristics.
7. Its teaching language.
8. Its equipment/environment assumptions.
9. Which movements are realistically camera-observable.
10. Which virtual tools make sense.
11. Where human coach judgment is especially important.

## Future packs
Design extension hooks for, but do not implement yet:
- conditioning / HIIT
- agility
- plyometrics
- jump rope
- dance conditioning
- parkour
- boxing / shadow boxing
- balance / coordination
- recovery

These future packs should plug into the same athlete model and canonical movement schema.

## Depth rule
A discipline is not considered supported because its name appears in a dropdown. A deep pack requires meaningful movement definitions, progression logic, programming rules, teaching cues, and validation.

HASHIRA should prefer four deep packs over twenty shallow ones.
