# HASHIRA Constitution

## Mission
HASHIRA is an intelligent movement training system. Its purpose is to help people train, learn movement, progress, and work with coaches using software that understands training rather than merely displaying workouts.

## Permanent principles

### 1. Movement first
Training quality comes before gamification, community, AI, visual effects, streaks, XP, or social features. Those systems may motivate and support training, but they must never define the product more strongly than movement itself.

### 2. Complex engine, simple experience
The engine may reason about training history, movement exposure, recovery, readiness, soreness, equipment, available time, environment, discipline, volume, intensity, coordination, progression, and preferences. The athlete should still see a simple answer: what to do today, why it was chosen, and how to start.

### 3. Knowledge before AI
HASHIRA must not use an LLM as a random workout generator. Canonical exercise definitions, progression trees, discipline rules, programming constraints, recovery logic, and safety boundaries must exist independently of generative AI. AI may interpret, explain, personalize, converse, and assist, but it must not invent the foundations of training.

### 4. Coach remains the authority
HASHIRA augments qualified coaches rather than replacing them. Coaches may override recommendations. When the system detects a potentially questionable programming choice, it should usually surface a reasoned consideration instead of blocking the coach.

### 5. Software first
The core product must deliver meaningful value using hardware people already own: phone, camera, microphone, speaker, screen, sensors, and internet connection. Proprietary hardware may be optional later but must not be required for the core experience.

### 6. One athlete model
Smart Workout, Movement Lab, HASHIRA Live, progression, coach tools, virtual training objects, and training history must converge on one athlete model. Do not create disconnected copies of goals, progress, or workout history.

### 7. One movement knowledge system
A canonical movement definition should eventually power Smart Workout, the exercise library, coach programming, progressions, Movement Lab, rep counting, vision analysis, virtual tools, and live sessions. Do not create separate incompatible definitions of the same exercise for each feature.

### 8. Teach, do not merely score
Computer vision should not behave like a constant error alarm. Corrections should account for confidence, severity, timing, and learning value. Minor technique opportunities may be summarized after a set; important issues may deserve a short live cue. The goal is understanding and improvement, not punishment.

### 9. No medical diagnosis
HASHIRA may respond conservatively to user-reported pain, soreness, movement limitations, and observable movement relationships. It must not diagnose injuries or medical conditions. Pain should trigger options such as stop, modify, reduce intensity, or seek an appropriate professional.

### 10. Quality before library size
A smaller library of deeply modeled movements is more valuable than thousands of shallow exercise names. Every important movement should eventually capture what it is, why it is used, when it fits, how to teach it, what precedes it, what follows it, its demands, fatigue cost, constraints, and camera observability.

### 11. Explain recommendations
Whenever practical, HASHIRA should answer **Why this workout?** and **Why this exercise?** in clear athlete-friendly language. Trust comes from understandable recommendations.

### 12. Preserve training intent when adapting
If a session changes because the athlete has less time, no equipment, soreness, a small room, or lower readiness, HASHIRA should preserve the purpose of the original session rather than replacing it with unrelated exercises.

### 13. Gamification is a progression layer
Ranks, XP, quests, streaks, missions, and Adventures may remain part of HASHIRA's identity. They sit on top of serious training rather than turning the product into an RPG that happens to contain workouts.

### 14. Human coaching and machine intelligence coexist
HASHIRA should become strongest where software intelligence and real human coaching meet: the engine prepares, observes, explains, and remembers; the coach teaches, judges context, motivates, and makes final decisions.

## Category
HASHIRA should not position itself as a smaller Fitbod, Tempo, Tonal, Freeletics, EGYM, or Zoom. Its category is **Intelligent Movement Training**: programming intelligence + movement knowledge + camera understanding + virtual training tools + real human coaching.
